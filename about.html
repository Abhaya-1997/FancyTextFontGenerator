How Our Unicode Conversion Works

Many visitors wonder whether these are "real fonts". In computer science terms, they are not traditional font files (like .ttf or .otf). Instead, they are characters defined in the international Unicode Standard, specifically from blocks such as:

  • Mathematical Alphanumeric Symbols: Providing cursive, double-struck, fraktur/gothic, bold, and monospace letterforms.
  • Enclosed Alphanumerics: Providing circled and boxed numbers and alphabet characters.
  • Fullwidth Forms: Creating the aesthetic vaporwave spaced effect originally designed for Asian typesetting.
  • Combining Diacritical Marks: Producing strikethroughs, underlines, and decorative upper halos.

Accessibility & Ethical Usage

Because Unicode mathematical symbols carry distinct character semantics, screen readers for visually impaired users may announce characters as "Mathematical Bold Capital A" rather than just "A".

We strongly advocate for responsible formatting: use fancy fonts for emphasis words, headings, names, decorative quotes, and bio accents, while keeping long paragraphs of critical information in standard plain text.
