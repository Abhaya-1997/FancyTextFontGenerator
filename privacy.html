Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 7, 2026

At Fancy Text Font Generator (accessible via fancytextfontgenerator.com, referred to as "we", "our", or "the Service"), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance. This comprehensive Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by our website and explains how we protect your digital rights.

Essential Summary: Your text input is processed 100% locally within your device browser. We do NOT store, log, inspect, or transmit the text you type into the generator.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike tools that send your keystrokes to a remote backend server for formatting, our Unicode conversion engine operates entirely client-side using JavaScript. Whatever you enter into the input box—whether it is a gaming nickname, a personal bio, or sensitive text—never leaves your computer or mobile device.

2. Standard Log Files

Fancy Text Font Generator follows standard web hosting protocols utilizing log files. These files log visitors when they access website pages. All standard web hosts perform this as part of infrastructure health and hosting service analytics. The information collected by log files includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and version
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and time stamps
  • Referring and exit pages
  • Number of clicks and navigational paths

None of this information is linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The sole purpose of this data is to analyze server performance, administer the site, debug technical errors, and ensure optimal delivery speeds.

3. Cookies and Browser Local Storage

Like most modern websites, we use basic storage mechanisms:

  • Browser Local Storage: Used strictly to remember your preferences (such as your chosen Dark or Light theme mode and your bookmarked ⭐ Favorites list). This data remains strictly in your browser and is never sent to our servers.
  • Standard Session Cookies: May be utilized to maintain basic browsing preferences and measure anonymized site traffic.
